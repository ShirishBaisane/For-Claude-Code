About Clicon Climate Control Solutions

A commercial vehicle cooling and transport refrigeration service partner focused on vehicle uptime, cooling reliability and preventive maintenance.

Company Overview

Who we are

Clicon Climate Control Solutions Private Limited is an official dealer of Carrier Transicold India, handling installation and aftermarket service of reefer air-conditioning and commercial vehicle refrigeration systems. We work with fleet operators, cold-chain logistics companies, pharmaceutical transporters, food and frozen-goods distributors, dairy and ice-cream logistics operators, and independent commercial vehicle owners across Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad.

While Carrier Transicold is our authorised OEM partnership, our public service capability is not limited to a single refrigeration brand — we support commercial vehicle AC and transport refrigeration systems from multiple manufacturers wherever technically applicable. Specific OEM expertise and part-level detail can be discussed directly during a technical consultation.

Our Mission

Vehicle uptime you can rely on

Our mission is to reduce breakdown-related downtime for commercial and refrigerated vehicle operators by delivering accurate diagnosis, reliable repairs and structured preventive maintenance — so fleets keep moving and cold-chain cargo stays protected.

Commercial Cooling Challenges

The problems we help fleets solve

Commercial cooling failures rarely happen at a convenient time. Common challenges we see include:

Unplanned breakdowns

Reefer or AC failure mid-route, risking cargo, schedules and customer commitments.

Recurring gas top-ups

Frequent refrigerant refilling that masks an underlying leak instead of fixing it.

Inconsistent cooling

Temperature fluctuation that puts sensitive cargo — medicines, dairy, frozen food — at risk.

No maintenance history

Fleets without structured service records struggle to plan preventive maintenance.

Multiple vehicle brands

Mixed fleets need a service partner capable of handling different systems reliably.

Limited technical diagnosis

Symptom-only servicing that replaces parts without confirming the actual root cause.

Our Service Philosophy

Inspect → Diagnose → Restore

Every service call follows the same disciplined process, so repairs address the actual cause of the fault rather than just the symptom.

1. Inspect

We examine the cooling / refrigeration system — compressor, condenser, evaporator, refrigerant pressure, electrical connections and controls.

2. Diagnose

We identify the probable root cause — not just the visible symptom — before recommending any repair.

3. Restore

We repair, service and verify cooling performance — and recommend preventive maintenance or AMC where appropriate.
