1. Information We Collect

When you submit the service enquiry form on this website, we collect the information you provide: name, company name (optional), mobile number, email address (optional), vehicle type, number of vehicles, service required, location and your message. We use the information you submit to respond to your service enquiry, contact you about your request, and maintain a record of leads generated through this website. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Enquiry data is stored securely on our hosting provider (Hostinger) and is accessible only to authorised Clicon personnel. We intend to progressively integrate this data with our internal customer and service-management systems.
