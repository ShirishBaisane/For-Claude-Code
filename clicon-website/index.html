About Clicon

A service partner focused on vehicle uptime

Clicon Climate Control Solutions Private Limited is an official dealer of Carrier Transicold India, offering installation and aftermarket service of reefer air-conditioning and commercial vehicle refrigeration systems. Our technicians follow a diagnosis-first approach — Inspect → Diagnose → Restore — so vehicles are repaired correctly the first time, not just re-charged with gas and sent back on the road.

We work with fleet operators, cold-chain logistics companies, pharmaceutical transporters, food & frozen goods distributors, dairy & ice-cream logistics operators and independent commercial vehicle owners across Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad.

FAQs

Common questions from fleet operators

Do you only service Carrier Transicold units?

We are the official dealer for Carrier Transicold India for installation and aftermarket service, and we also support commercial vehicle AC and reefer systems from other brands wherever technically applicable. Contact our team to confirm feasibility for your specific unit.

Do you offer Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) for fleets?

Yes. We offer Fleet AMC plans with scheduled inspections, preventive checks, maintenance history tracking and priority support for operators running multiple vehicles.

Can you support an emergency breakdown?

Call or WhatsApp us directly with your location and vehicle details. Emergency support is provided subject to location, vehicle type and technician availability.

Which areas do you currently service?

We are based in Pune / PCMC and serve surrounding industrial and logistics areas. See our Service Areas page for the full list.

How do I request a quotation?

Use the enquiry form, call, or WhatsApp us with your vehicle type, issue and location, and our service team will get back to you.

Ready to get your fleet's cooling system inspected?

Book a service inspection or request a Fleet AMC quotation — our team will respond promptly.
